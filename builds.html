Builds Guide

Optimal builds for each class and playstyle.

Mage Builds

Primal Fire Mage

Maximize fire damage with burn effects.

Key Abilities

  • • Fireball / Inferno
  • • Burn mastery
  • • Elemental vulnerability

Playstyle: Ranged damage dealer, keep distance

Veilweaver Support

Keep your team alive with barriers and healing.

Key Abilities

  • • Barrier / Mana Shield
  • • Healing surge
  • • Spirit summoning

Playstyle: Support, maintain team survival

Warrior Builds

Champion Tank

Draw enemy aggro and protect your team.

Key Abilities

  • • Shield Wall / Taunt
  • • Defensive stance
  • • Team protection

Playstyle: Frontline, absorb damage

Destroyer DPS

Massive damage with two-handed weapons.

Key Abilities

  • • Heavy swings / Cleave
  • • Momentum builder
  • • Ground slam

Playstyle: Slow but devastating attacks

Rogue Builds

Assassin Crit

Maximum critical damage from stealth.

Key Abilities

  • • Backstab / Ambush
  • • Critical chance boost
  • • Stealth regeneration

Playstyle: Stealth kill, high burst damage

Artificer Utility

Traps, bombs, and ranged poison attacks.

Key Abilities

  • • Poison bombs
  • • Caltrops / Traps
  • • Ranged daggers

Playstyle: Crowd control, sustained damage

Team Composition Tips

  • • Always bring a healer - Harding or Emmerich for support
  • • Tank is essential - Davrin can hold aggro
  • • Mix damage types - Physical + Magic for versatility
  • • Match elements to enemies - Some enemies are weak to specific elements
