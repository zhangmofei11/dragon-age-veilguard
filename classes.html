Classes Guide

Choose your class and master your abilities.

Base Classes

Mage

Master of arcane magic. Devastating elemental attacks and powerful buffs.

  • • Fire, Ice, Lightning spells
  • • Healing and barrier magic
  • • High damage, low defense

Warrior

Master of combat. Heavy weapons and shield techniques.

  • • Sword and shield combat
  • • Taunt and protect allies
  • • High defense, steady damage

Rogue

Master of stealth. Fast attacks and critical strikes.

  • • Dual wielding daggers
  • • Backstab and stealth
  • • High crit, glass cannon

Mage Specializations

Primal

Elemental magic focused on fire, ice, and lightning.

  • • Inferno: Fire-based attacks
  • • Winter's Grasp: Ice spells
  • • Tempest: Lightning storm

Spellblade

Hybrid mage that combines magic with melee combat.

  • • Magic-infused melee attacks
  • • Spell combos
  • • Close-range powerhouse

Veilweaver

Manipulates the Fade for reality-bending abilities.

  • • Barrier manipulation
  • • Mind control
  • • Spirit summoning

Warrior Specializations

Champion

  • • Shield expertise
  • • Taunt enemies
  • • Team survivability
