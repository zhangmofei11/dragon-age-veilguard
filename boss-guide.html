BOSS BATTLE GUIDE

Strategies for all major boss encounters

🐉 DRAGON BOSSES

🔥

Infernal Drake

Fire dragon encountered in Act 2

Weakness: Ice, Knockback
Strategy: Avoid fire breath, attack during dive
❄️

Frost Wyrm

Ice dragon in Northern mountains

Weakness: Fire, Lightning
Strategy: Stay mobile, break ice armor first
⚡

Storm Serpent

Electric dragon in storm region

Weakness: Ground attacks
Strategy: Insulate from lightning, strike when grounded
💀

Ancient Dragon

Final dragon, endgame content

Weakness: Varies by phase
Strategy: Phase 1: Break scales, Phase 2: Dodge aoe

👁️ ELVEN GOD BOSSES

🌙

The Forgotten

Corrupted Elven god, Act 1 boss

Weakness: Light magic
Strategy: Break barrier, attack during recovery
☀️

Elven Sun God

Act 3 final boss encounter

Weakness: Dark magic
Strategy: Counter beam attacks, dodge summon adds

💡 BOSS FIGHT TIPS

Preparation

  • • Bring potions & gear
  • • Check boss weakness
  • • Upgrade weapons first

Combat

  • • Learn attack patterns
  • • Dodge, don't tank
  • • Focus on weaknesses

Teamwork

  • • Use companion abilities
  • • Coordinate combos
  • • Heal at right moments
